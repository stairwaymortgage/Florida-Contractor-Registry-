About Aceca Construction

Aceca Construction, Inc. is a Florida-licensed general contracting business headquartered in Davie, in Broward County. The business was incorporated in September 2012 and has operated continuously under the same qualifying agent since then — a level of stability uncommon in an industry where most businesses dissolve within seven years.

The qualifying agent, Cristian F. Acero, holds an unusually broad license stack for a single contractor: a Certified General Contractor designation (CGC1520921) granting statewide authority for both residential and commercial construction; a Certified Roofing Contractor designation (CCC1330257) added in 2013; and a Certified Plumbing Contractor designation (CFC1432705) added in 2024. This combination allows the business to self-perform across structural, roofing, and plumbing scopes without subcontracting — a capability typically found only in larger commercial construction firms.

The Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB) classifies general contractors under Fla. Stat. § 489.105(3)(a), authorizing them to perform "construction, remodeling, repair, or improvement... in the construction or remodeling of any building" without limitation on size, height, or scope. Roofing and plumbing certifications are issued under § 489.105(3)(e) and § 489.105(3)(g) respectively.

According to public DBPR records as of May 2026, all five active licenses associated with Aceca Construction are current with active secondary status and no public disciplinary history on file.

Common Questions About This Contractor

Is Aceca Construction, Inc. licensed in Florida?
Yes. Aceca Construction holds five active certified contractor licenses with the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation, qualified by Cristian F. Acero. Primary license (CGC1520921) has been current and active since September 2012.
Where does Aceca Construction operate?
Aceca Construction is headquartered in Davie, Broward County. Because the business holds Certified state licenses (not Registered), it has statewide authority and can perform work in any Florida county without additional licensing.
