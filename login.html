Contractor Sign In

Sign in to your profile.

Enter the email address you used when you claimed your profile. We'll send you a sign-in link.

Haven't claimed your profile yet? Find your business on the registry and start the claim process — it takes about five minutes.

No Password Required

We don't use passwords.

Every time you sign in, we send a one-time link to your email address. Click the link and you're signed in. No password to remember, no password to forget, no password to steal.

  • The sign-in link is valid for 15 minutes
  • It can only be used once
  • If someone else opens the link, your session is revoked automatically
Trouble signing in? Email support@floridacontractorregistry.com and we'll help you sort it out within one business day.
