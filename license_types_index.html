Florida contractor license types.

Florida issues 29 distinct contractor license categories, each defining the specific scope of work that holder is legally allowed to perform. Understanding the difference matters — a roofing license doesn't cover electrical work, and a residential contractor cannot legally build a four-story commercial building.
