Honest Answers

What contractors usually want to know.

Why is my profile already on this site?
Florida contractor licenses are public record under Chapter 119 of the Florida Statutes. The DBPR publishes a weekly export of all active licenses. We aggregate that data into a searchable directory so homeowners can verify licenses before signing contracts. Your profile exists whether you claim it or not — claiming it lets you control what visitors see.
Can I remove my profile entirely?
We can't remove public DBPR data while your license is active — that information is in the public record and republication is protected by law. What we CAN do: if there's a factual error (wrong address, misspelled name, wrong license type), we'll fix it once you verify ownership. Claim your profile, contact support, and we'll get it corrected within one business day.
Is this affiliated with the State of Florida?
No. Florida Contractor Registry is a private commercial directory operated by Florida Contractor Registry LLC. We're not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the State of Florida, the DBPR, or the Construction Industry Licensing Board. We republish public data — that's all. For official license verification, the State maintains myfloridalicense.com.
What happens to my ID after verification?
It's stored privately in encrypted cloud storage with restricted access. Only the admin reviewer (one person) can view it during the verification process. We don't share it, sell it, or use it for any other purpose. Once your claim is approved or rejected, the image is retained for 90 days for audit purposes, then permanently deleted.
Will you sell my contact info?
No. We don't sell contractor contact data. Period. Homeowner inquiries that come through your profile are routed directly to you. If you don't claim your profile, no one can contact you through this site at all — the public DBPR record doesn't include phone or email.
What's the catch?
The free tier covers everything most contractors need. The optional Featured tier ($29/month) is how we keep the site running. We also offer a separate website service ($199/month) through a partner brand if you need a real website — that's optional and not tied to your registry profile. No other monetization on the contractor side.
How long does verification really take?
Most claims are approved within 24 hours. Many within the same business day. Verification is done by a real human, not an algorithm — that means an actual person reviews your ID against DBPR records before approving. We accept this trade-off (slower than instant) because it keeps the registry trustworthy: a "Verified" badge has to actually mean something.
